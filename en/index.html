Free Android protection tool

A barrier before the impulse becomes a bet.

AntiBet Alert helps identify and block access to supported gambling websites. It can also notify someone you trust by email, only when you choose to enable that feature.

Download on Google Play How it works

Free, no ads, no subscription, no mandatory account.

AntiBet Alert is an additional layer of protection. It does not promise to block every gambling website, app, advertisement, or access method, and it does not replace professional care or support from people you trust.

Protection at the moment it matters

Identifies supported websites

It combines known domains and other address signals to identify gambling websites.

Interrupts access

When a supported website is identified, an intervention appears and prevents access.

Optional support partner

You can choose someone you trust to receive an email alert after a detected attempt.

Privacy on your device

Usage data stays on your phone. The app has no advertising and does not sell your data.

How it works

1

You enable protection

The app explains why Android Accessibility is needed and requests your consent before opening system settings.

2

The domain is checked on your phone

The Accessibility Service reads only the domain shown in the address bar of compatible browsers, not passwords, messages, forms, or page content.

3

The app creates a barrier

If the address is recognized, AntiBet Alert displays an intervention and offers actions to close or temporarily block the website.

Support does not have to wait

The support-partner feature is optional. When enabled, the person you choose receives the website name and time by email. They do not receive your passwords, messages, complete browsing history, or page content.

Privacy and Accessibility

The Accessibility Service is used with your consent. It allows AntiBet Alert to read the domain displayed by compatible browsers and show the intervention at the right time. The app is intended for voluntary use on your own device, not hidden monitoring of another person.

  • No mandatory registration.
  • No advertising SDK used to monetize your attention.
  • Usage records remain on the device.
  • Email features are optional and configured by the user.

International expansion, handled responsibly

AntiBet Alert was created in Brazil and its English and Spanish experiences are being prepared for Colombia, Peru, and Pennsylvania in the United States. Website and app availability depends on the user's Google Play country.

Detection coverage varies by market. A translated interface does not mean every local gambling domain or app is already covered. Check the current Google Play listing before installing and use AntiBet Alert together with local self-exclusion, trusted support, and professional care when needed.

How supported addresses are recognized

Known domains

The app can recognize addresses already included in its local detection resources.

Address patterns

Signals in a domain can help identify less obvious addresses.

Redirects and technical checks

Additional signals can help evaluate the path to an address.

Analysis of unclear addresses

A local analysis layer helps assess domains that do not match simple patterns.

Features designed for a real moment

Immediate intervention

The app interrupts a supported access attempt instead of waiting for a later report.

Temporary blocking

Depending on the flow and settings available in the app, you can choose an additional temporary barrier.

Local progress

Optional local records can help you notice patterns and follow your own progress.

Support by email

A trusted person can receive the limited alert you configure.

A complementary layer, not a complete solution

No app can guarantee that every website, app, advertisement, mirror, redirect or access method will be blocked. AntiBet Alert should be combined with a trusted support network, professional care and official resources available in your location.

Compatibility and availability

  • Android 7.0 or later.
  • Compatible browsers can include Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet, Edge and similar browsers.
  • In-app browsers may behave differently after external updates.
  • There is no iPhone version at this time.
  • International detection coverage is still being validated for each market.

Information and support

If betting is affecting your money, health, relationships or safety, seek help from someone you trust and a qualified local service. AntiBet Alert is not an emergency service or a medical treatment.

Pennsylvania: official problem-gambling support is available through the Pennsylvania DDAP. The state also provides information about voluntary self-exclusion. Confirm current conditions on the official pages.

About the project

AntiBet Alert is a personal project created by software developer Brenda Bispo. It began in October 2025 after a family experience with gambling addiction and was launched in July 2026. The project is intended to remain free, without ads, while usage information stays on the user's device.

Important questions

Does it block every gambling website?

No. Websites and access methods change frequently. AntiBet Alert is one complementary protection layer.

Is it a medical treatment?

No. It is a personal technology tool and does not replace medical, psychological, legal, or financial assistance.

Is it really free?

Yes. AntiBet Alert is free, has no ads, and requires no subscription.

How it works

1

You enable protection

The app explains why Android Accessibility is needed and requests your consent before opening system settings.

2

The domain is checked on your phone

The Accessibility Service reads only the domain shown in the address bar of compatible browsers, not passwords, messages, forms, or page content.

3

The app creates a barrier

If the address is recognized, AntiBet Alert displays an intervention and offers actions to close or temporarily block the website.

Support does not have to wait

The support-partner feature is optional. When enabled, the person you choose receives the website name and time by email. They do not receive your passwords, messages, complete browsing history, or page content.

Privacy and Accessibility

The Accessibility Service is used with your consent. It allows AntiBet Alert to read the domain displayed by compatible browsers and show the intervention at the right time. The app is intended for voluntary use on your own device, not hidden monitoring of another person.

  • No mandatory registration.
  • No advertising SDK used to monetize your attention.
  • Usage records remain on the device.
  • Email features are optional and configured by the user.

International expansion, handled responsibly

AntiBet Alert was created in Brazil and its English and Spanish experiences are being prepared for Colombia, Peru, and Pennsylvania in the United States. Website and app availability depends on the user's Google Play country.

Detection coverage varies by market. A translated interface does not mean every local gambling domain or app is already covered. Check the current Google Play listing before installing and use AntiBet Alert together with local self-exclusion, trusted support, and professional care when needed.

Important questions

Does it block every gambling website?

No. Websites and access methods change frequently. AntiBet Alert is one complementary protection layer.

Is it a medical treatment?

No. It is a personal technology tool and does not replace medical, psychological, legal, or financial assistance.

Is it really free?

Yes. AntiBet Alert is free, has no ads, and requires no subscription.

Support partner

A trusted person can be part of your protection plan, with your permission and clear limits.

AntiBet Alert content · Reviewed August 10, 2026

The support partner is optional. When you enable it, AntiBet Alert can send an email to the person you choose after a supported access attempt is identified.

What the email can contain

  • The name of the supported website.
  • The time of the detected attempt.
  • A reminder that the message was sent by the feature you configured.

What is not shared

The feature is not a live monitor. It does not send passwords, messages, payment information, page content or a complete browsing history to your support partner.

Set it up thoughtfully

  1. Choose someone who understands that the goal is support, not punishment.
  2. Explain what the email means before enabling it.
  3. Confirm that the email address is correct.
  4. Discuss how you would like the person to respond.

This is not an emergency service. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis service. A support-partner email may be delayed or fail.

Privacy and consent

Use the feature only for yourself and with the other person's knowledge. You control whether it is enabled and which address receives the email.

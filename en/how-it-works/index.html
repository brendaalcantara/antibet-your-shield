How AntiBet Alert works

AntiBet Alert creates distance between an impulse and the next action through local checks, an intervention and optional support.

AntiBet Alert content · Reviewed August 10, 2026

Basic flow: the app reads the domain shown in compatible browsers, checks signals associated with gambling websites and, when a supported address is identified, shows an intervention and interrupts access.

From access to intervention

1

Limited domain reading

With Accessibility enabled by your consent, the app reads only the main address shown in the browser bar of compatible browsers.

2

Local detection checks

The address is compared with known domains and evaluated using technical signals and patterns. More than one check can contribute to the result.

3

An intervention at the critical moment

When a supported attempt is identified, the app displays an intervention over the browser to interrupt automatic navigation.

4

Access interruption and local record

The access is interrupted and the domain and time may be recorded on the device.

5

Optional support

If you enable it, an email alert can be sent to a trusted person configured by you.

Detection in plain language

Known websites

Recognizes addresses already identified as related to gambling.

Domain patterns

Looks at signals in the name and structure of the address.

Less obvious domains

An additional analysis layer helps evaluate addresses that simple rules do not recognize.

Technical signals

Certificate, redirects and other checks help understand the access path.

No layer is infallible

Websites change addresses, new apps appear and ads can open different paths. AntiBet Alert may miss an address or flag one incorrectly. Use it with other forms of protection.

Current compatibility

  • Android 7.0 or later.
  • Compatible browsers such as Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet, Edge and similar browsers.
  • Compatibility with in-app browsers can vary after external updates.

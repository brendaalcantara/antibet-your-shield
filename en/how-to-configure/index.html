How to configure AntiBet Alert

Follow the essential steps to activate protection on your own Android device.

AntiBet Alert content · Reviewed August 10, 2026

To get started: install the app from Google Play, read the disclosure, grant the required Android permission with your consent and choose the optional support features that make sense for you.

Before you begin

  • Use Android 7.0 or later.
  • Install the official app from Google Play.
  • Have a trusted person in mind if you want to use support-partner alerts.

Setup steps

1

Read the explanation

The app explains what it needs and why. Continue only if the use makes sense to you.

2

Enable Accessibility with consent

Android Accessibility is used to read only the domain displayed in the address bar of compatible browsers. It is not used to read passwords, messages, forms or page content.

3

Allow the intervention to appear

Android may ask for permission to show the intervention over the browser. Grant it only if you want the app to interrupt detected access.

4

Protect background operation

Some phones restrict background activity. Follow the app's guidance if the system asks you to adjust battery optimization.

5

Choose optional support

You can configure email reports or a trusted support partner. These features are optional and controlled by you.

Use it for yourself. AntiBet Alert is designed for voluntary protection on your own device. Do not use it to monitor another person without consent.

If protection stops

Open the app and check the permission notices. Browser updates, battery settings and Android manufacturer settings can affect operation. The app cannot guarantee uninterrupted coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about what the app does, what it does not do and how to use it responsibly.

AntiBet Alert content · Reviewed August 10, 2026

AntiBet Alert is a complementary Android tool. It creates an additional barrier, but it cannot guarantee that every gambling website or access route will be blocked.

Is AntiBet Alert free?

Yes. It is free, has no ads and does not require a subscription or mandatory account.

Does it block every gambling website?

No. Domains, apps and advertising paths change. Coverage can vary by browser, version and market.

Why does it request Accessibility?

With your consent, the service allows the app to read only the domain shown in the address bar of compatible browsers and display an intervention.

Does it read my messages or passwords?

No. The app is not designed to read messages, passwords, forms, payment details or page content.

Can someone else monitor me with it?

No. It is intended for voluntary personal protection. Do not configure it to monitor another person without consent.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at this time. The current app is for Android 7.0 or later.

Is it a treatment?

No. It does not replace professional health, financial, legal or crisis support.

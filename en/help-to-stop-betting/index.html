Help to stop betting

A technology tool can help with one moment. Recovery usually needs a wider support network.

AntiBet Alert content · Reviewed August 10, 2026

If you are trying to stop, start with the next safe step. Close the access, tell someone you trust and protect money needed for food, housing and essential bills.

Small steps that can help

  1. Do not try to recover losses by betting again.
  2. Tell a trusted person what is happening and ask for practical support.
  3. Use self-exclusion or blocking tools available in your location.
  4. Reduce easy access to payment methods while you build support.
  5. Look for qualified mental-health, addiction or financial counselling.

How AntiBet Alert fits

The app can add a barrier on Android and, if you choose, email a support partner after a detected attempt. It does not replace treatment, peer groups, self-exclusion or emergency help.

Pennsylvania, United States

The Pennsylvania Department of Drug and Alcohol Programs lists confidential problem-gambling support and the 1-800-GAMBLER helpline. It also provides official voluntary self-exclusion information. Check the official pages for current conditions: treatment and support and self-exclusion.

Emergency: if you may hurt yourself or someone else, call your local emergency number now or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for an app or email response.

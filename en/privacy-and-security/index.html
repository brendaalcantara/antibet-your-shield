Privacy by design

Privacy and security

The app is designed to keep usage information on your device and give you control over optional email features.

AntiBet Alert content · Reviewed August 10, 2026

In short: the app reads only the domain shown in the address bar of compatible browsers when you consent to Accessibility. Usage records stay on the device, and email alerts are optional.

What the app can read

  • The domain displayed in the address bar of compatible browsers.
  • The time of a detected access, when the app records it locally.
  • Information needed to show an intervention over the browser.

What it does not read

  • Passwords, messages, private conversations or contact lists.
  • Form fields, payment details or the contents of the page.
  • Another person's device or activity without their consent.

Accessibility is used with your consent. Android provides the permission; AntiBet Alert uses it for the limited purpose explained in the app and this page.

Optional email features

If you configure email reports or a support partner, the app sends the message to the address you choose. The support alert can include the website name and time of the detected attempt. It is not a full browsing-history report.

Your controls

  • You can turn optional features off in the app.
  • You can remove stored local data using the app's controls.
  • You can disable the Android permission in system settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

What we promise

AntiBet Alert has no ads, does not sell usage data and does not require a mandatory account. Read the Privacy Policy for the formal terms.
